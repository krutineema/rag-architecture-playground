# Payments Approval Policy

## Standard payments

Payments below £10,000 may be approved through the normal operational process.
The initiating employee must still complete the required beneficiary and fraud checks.

## High-value payments

Payments of £10,000 or more require approval from an authorised payment approver.
Payments of £50,000 or more require two independent approvals.

## Emergency payments

Emergency payments may use the expedited process, but the normal approval evidence must be recorded retrospectively.
