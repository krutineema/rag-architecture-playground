# Mortgage Lending Policy

## Standard lending limits

The standard maximum loan-to-value ratio for a first-time buyer is 90%.
For other residential borrowers, the standard maximum loan-to-value ratio is 80%.

## Exceptions

Applications above the standard LTV limit require enhanced underwriting review.
The reviewer must document the reason for the exception and obtain approval from a senior credit officer.

## Affordability

All mortgage applications must pass the affordability assessment before an offer is issued.
The assessment considers verified income, existing commitments and the proposed monthly payment.
