# Customer Data Retention Policy

## Retention period

Customer account records must normally be retained for seven years after the relationship ends.
Transaction records must also be retained for seven years unless a longer legal or regulatory requirement applies.

## Legal holds

Where a legal hold is active, relevant records must not be deleted even if the normal retention period has expired.
The legal or compliance team is responsible for releasing the hold.

## Disposal

Records that have reached the end of their retention period should be securely deleted according to the approved disposal process.
