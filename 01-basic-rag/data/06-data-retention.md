# Data Retention Policy

## Customer records
Customer records must be retained only for as long as required by applicable legal, regulatory and business requirements.

## Retention periods
The exact retention period depends on the record type and the applicable retention schedule. Staff must use the current retention schedule rather than relying on an informal period.

## Deletion
Records that have reached the end of their approved retention period should be deleted or securely disposed of unless a valid legal hold applies.

## Legal hold
A legal hold overrides normal deletion for records within the scope of the hold until the hold is released.
