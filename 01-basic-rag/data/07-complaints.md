# Customer Complaints Policy

## Making a complaint
Customers may raise complaints through the firm's supported channels, including online, telephone and written channels.

## Complaint handling
Complaints must be recorded and handled according to the firm's complaints procedure. Staff should provide clear information about the next steps.

## Escalation
Where a complaint cannot be resolved through the normal process, it must be escalated according to the applicable complaints procedure and time limits.

## Records
Complaint records must be retained in accordance with the applicable data-retention requirements.
