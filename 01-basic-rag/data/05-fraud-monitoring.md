# Fraud Monitoring Policy

## Transaction monitoring
Transactions are monitored for patterns that may indicate fraud or account takeover.

## Suspicious activity
When a transaction triggers a high-confidence fraud rule, the transaction may be temporarily blocked while the case is reviewed.

## Customer contact
Where customer contact is required, staff must follow the approved authentication process before discussing account-specific information.

## Escalation
Confirmed or suspected fraud cases must be escalated to the fraud operations team according to the incident severity.
