# Password Reset Policy

## Self-service reset
Customers who forget their online banking password may use the self-service password reset journey. The customer must successfully complete the required identity checks before a new password can be established.

## Failed identity checks
If the identity checks fail, the self-service reset must not proceed. The customer should be directed to the supported recovery process.

## Temporary restrictions
Repeated failed password-reset attempts may trigger a temporary restriction. The restriction is intended to protect the account from unauthorised access.

## Support escalation
Customer support may assist with recovery, but support staff must not bypass the identity-verification controls.
