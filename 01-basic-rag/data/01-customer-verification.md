# Customer Verification Policy

## Identity verification
All new retail customers must complete identity verification before an account can be activated. The verification process must validate the customer's identity using the approved identity-verification provider.

## Failed verification
If identity verification fails, the customer must not be activated automatically. The case should be routed to the customer verification team for manual review.

## High-risk customers
Additional verification may be required when the customer is classified as high risk. The compliance team may request supporting evidence before activation.

## Verification records
Verification outcomes and supporting evidence must be retained according to the firm's applicable data-retention policy.
