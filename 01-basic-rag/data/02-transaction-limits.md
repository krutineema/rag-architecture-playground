# Transaction Limits Policy

## Standard limits
New retail customers are subject to a daily card payment limit of £1,000 for the first 30 days after account activation.

## Increased limits
Customers may request a higher limit after the initial 30-day period. Requests must pass the firm's risk checks before the limit is changed.

## High-risk customers
Higher-risk customers may have lower transaction limits imposed by the risk team.

## Exceptions
Any exception to the standard transaction limit must be approved by an authorised risk manager and recorded in the customer account.
