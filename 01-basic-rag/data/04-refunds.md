# Refund Policy

## Card purchase refunds
Refunds for eligible card purchases should normally be returned to the original payment method.

## Refund timing
A merchant may take several business days to complete a refund. Customers should not be promised an exact settlement time unless the payment provider has confirmed it.

## Disputed transactions
A customer who believes a card transaction is unauthorised should follow the firm's transaction-dispute process rather than treating the case as a normal merchant refund.

## Exceptions
Manual refund exceptions require approval from an authorised operations manager.
